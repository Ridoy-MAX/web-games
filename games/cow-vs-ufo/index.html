How to Play

  • Eat snacks 🍎🌽🍀 to clear the level. Golden corn on the balloon rafts is worth 5×.
  • Dodge the tractor beam. A red ring means it's about to fire. Stay in the beam and you get abducted.
  • Hide in barns to shake the UFO. Hide too long and it steals the roof.
  • Jump over the spinning glove, fences and hay. Watch the red circles: anvils are coming.
  • Don't fall off the island. Gravity is not your friend.
  • Moo to scare chickens, including the very large one.
